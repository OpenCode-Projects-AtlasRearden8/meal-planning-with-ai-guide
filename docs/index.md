# Meal Planning with Free AI: A Beginner's Manual

*Part of the ASDALE DYNAMICS beginner AI series, explained like a friend would.*

> **Important:** This manual is **education only**. It teaches you how to use AI tools to organize your week. It is **not nutrition advice** and never will be. If you have medical or dietary needs, talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian (like Dylan, the author, does with his own providers).

## Who this is for

- You want weekly dinners decided *before* 5 p.m. panic mode.
- You want to try **free** AI helpers — ChatGPT's free tier, Claude's free tier, Google Gemini's free tier, or a fully local option (Ollama).
- Your evenings are low energy and picky eaters are real people in your house (they might be you).
- You want your food situation to stay **your** data — nothing uploaded, nothing sold, nothing required.

## What you need

- Any device with a web browser. That's it.
- (Optional, for Chapter 5) A computer with at least 8 GB of RAM running Windows, macOS, or Linux.

## The five-minute version

1. Open any free AI chat that doesn't need a subscription (free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or the fully-local Ollama).
2. **Pantry-first:** tell it what's already in your kitchen.
3. Ask for **7 dinners**, including 2 "short-order nights" that are easy even when you're running on empty.
4. Ask for the **grocery list in a checklist format** you can edit.
5. Copy the plan onto the [printable planner page](07-printable-planner.md). Done for the week.

## Ground rules we keep

- **Education, never nutrition advice.** Prompts in this manual ask AI for *logistics* (what to cook, when, with what ingredients) — not medical or diet recommendations, and never estimates of "ideal calories" for you.
- **Local-first.** Wherever possible, keep your household details on your device. Chapter 5 shows you how to do that completely offline.
- **You are the editor.** AI drafts, you decide. Every prompt in this manual ends with you pressing modify, not accept.
- **No shame design.** If you order pizza on a short-order night, that *was* the short-order plan working as intended. The system has slack built in on purpose.

## Privacy in 30 seconds

Everything a cloud AI chat sees is data that may be stored by the company running it. So the prompts in this manual are written **deliberately vague by default** ("some chicken, rice, and frozen vegetables") rather than pasting your entire household profile. If you want to give the AI richer context — allergies, preferences, budgets — Chapter 5's local setup with [Ollama](https://ollama.com) keeps those sentences on your own machine. See [docs/05-local-ai-with-ollama.md](05-local-ai-with-ollama.md) for the full walkthrough.
