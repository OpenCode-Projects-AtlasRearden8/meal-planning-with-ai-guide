The five-minute version

  1. Open any free AI chat — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini free tiers, or fully-local Ollama.
  2. Pantry-first: tell it what you already own.
  3. Ask for 7 dinners, including 2 easy “short-order nights”.
  4. Ask for the grocery list in checklist format.
  5. Copy it onto the printable planner. Done for the week.

Ground rules

  • Education, never nutrition advice. Prompts here ask AI for logistics only.
  • Local-first. Chapter 5 shows a fully offline setup so household details never leave your computer.
  • You are the editor. AI drafts; you decide.
  • No shame design. Short-order nights have slack built in on purpose.
